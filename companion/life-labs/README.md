# Life labs: Chapters 35–56

Begin with [the workbook](WORKBOOK.md). The [source packet](packet.json) contains authored fictional inputs. The [reference code](reference.py) performs arithmetic and rule checks without calling an AI provider. It is optional; every exercise can be completed manually.

Amounts and policies belong to these exercises. They are not current quotes, a venue policy, business advice or a record of observed customer behavior. Reference answers show the author's intended reasoning. They do not demonstrate that ChatGPT produced the answers or that a reader learned the skill.

Save your input, actual output, checks, corrections and changed-condition attempt. Record assistance. Use fictional or voluntarily selected non-sensitive information first. Share results only when you intend to share them.
