Does a refundable deposit necessarily become a permanent cost?	No. Its final treatment depends on the stated return terms.
What does a source filename establish?	A named file; it does not prove the contents were read.
A notice lists $24 membership and $15 deposit but leaves a possible fee unstated. What does $39 establish?	The sum of the two listed amounts, not a complete final cost.
How does a membership payment differ from a refundable deposit?	Membership buys membership; the deposit may be returned under stated terms.
